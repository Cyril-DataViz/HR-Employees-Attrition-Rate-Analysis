# HR-Employees-Attrition-Rate-Analysis
An analysis of employee demographics, workforce distribution and employee attrition using Power BI

**1. Project Overview**

This project analyses employee data to understand workforce demographics, employee distribution and attrition patterns.

**Dataset**

The dataset contains employee-related information used for workforce analysis.

**2. Business Objectives**

Understand the different causes for attrition.

Analyse employee attrition within different Age groups.

Identify department affected most by attrition.

Understand employee educational level with the most attrition rate.

Which employee work arrangement has the most attrition rate.

Provide recommendations for improving employee retention.

**Tool Used**
PowerBi

**Analysis Performed**

Attrition rate calculation

Causes for attrition

Attrition rate distribution by age group

Attrition rate distribution by department

Attrition rate distribution by Educational Level

Attrition rate distribution by work arrangement

**Key Findings**

**Causes of employee attrition.**

Better compensation is the leading recorded reason for employees leaving, accounting for 16 out of 60 exits. Career growth is second, with 13 exits.

Together, these two reasons account for 29 employees, or 48.33% of all recorded exits.

**Attrition Rate Distribution By Age Group.**

The Elderly has the highest attrition rate at 19.05%, Adults has the lowest rate at 6.18%, Youth has a rate of 11.61%, Young adults is at 10.98%

N.B. Age Group categories: 21-30(Youth), 31-40(Young Adult), 41-50(Adult), 51&above(Elderly).


**Attrition rate by distribution department**

Customer Support has the highest attrition rate at 15.66%. This means roughly 16 out of every 100 employees in that department are recorded as terminated, Operations follows at 13.56%.

Sales has the third-highest rate at 12.20%, Engineering has the lowest rate at 5.41%.

**Attrition rate distribution by Educational Level**

- Employees with Bachelor's degrees have the highest attrition rate at 12.25%.
 
- PhD holders follow at 11.32%.
  
- Employees with Master's degrees have the lowest rate at 6.63%.
  
- High School graduates have an attrition rate of 8.16%.

**Attrition rate distriburion by work arrangement**

Remote employees have the highest attrition rate at 11.61%.

On-site employees have a rate of 9.89%.

Hybrid employees have the lowest rate at 9.48%.

**Recommendations**

Improve compensation and benefits: Better compensation accounts for 26.67% of recorded exits. Management should compare salaries with similar positions in the industry and review whether employee rewards are competitive.

Investigate departments with high attrition: Customer Support, Operations and Sales should be prioritized. Conduct employee surveys and exit interviews to identify specific problems

Investigate whether remote employees receive adequate support and communication.
